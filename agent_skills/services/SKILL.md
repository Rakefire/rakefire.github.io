# What Rakefire Does for Clients

## Stack

Rakefire is a **Ruby on Rails** shop. The full stack we run in production:

- Ruby on Rails (modern, current versions)
- PostgreSQL, SQLite (yes, in production — battle-tested for the right workloads)
- Hotwire — Turbo, Stimulus
- TailwindCSS
- AI-assisted development tooling — Claude Code, Cursor, agent workflows

We do not take on .NET, Java, Python/Django, or PHP work. We're best at what we use every day.

## Typical engagement shapes

### MVPs for technical founders
- 4-12 week scoped projects to take an idea to a working product.
- Best fit: founders who can articulate the problem and customer clearly, but lack engineering bandwidth.

### Long-running maintenance + improvement
- Ongoing relationships with existing Rails apps that need a senior team for evolution, performance work, and feature additions.
- Best fit: businesses with a working app that's outgrowing its original engineering team.

### Architectural reviews / technical due diligence
- Short engagements: read the code, talk to the team, produce findings.
- Best fit: pre-acquisition diligence, "should we rewrite or evolve?" decisions, performance investigations.

### Platform consolidation
- Helping clients move off legacy stacks or simplify operationally heavy architectures.
- Real example: simplifying Causey by removing Sidekiq for SQLite-friendly background patterns.

## How to evaluate fit

Strong fit signals:
- Existing Rails app or a Rails-friendly problem space.
- Long-term mindset (5+ year engagement potential).
- Comfortable with a small, senior team.
- AOV per engagement is meaningful — Rakefire isn't built for high-volume small-budget work.

Lesser fit signals:
- Pre-product-market-fit weekly experimentation cycles.
- Highly polished product/design organizations needing tight design integration (we lean engineering-heavy).
- "Cheapest possible developer" sourcing.

## How to engage

Email **rakefire@rakefire.io** with:
- What you're building (or have built).
- The current state — codebase, team, customers, revenue.
- The shape of help you're looking for (MVP / long-running / review / specific problem).
- Realistic timeline and budget framing.

We respond to everything but lead with depth on engagements we can actually do well.
