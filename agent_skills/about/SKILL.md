# About Rakefire

## What it is

Rakefire is a small Ruby on Rails **agency and product studio** based in Athens, Ohio. We do two things:

1. **Build software for clients** — Rails apps, MVPs, and ongoing maintenance for businesses that need a small, senior team they can trust over multiple years.
2. **Ship our own SaaS** — products we built because we needed them ourselves and decided to keep going.

## Founder & principal

**Ricky Chilcott** has run Rakefire since 2011. Full-stack engineer and product-focused entrepreneur with 15+ years building and scaling SaaS platforms in Ruby on Rails. See https://www.rickychilcott.com for his personal site, blog, and resume.

## Operating philosophy

- **Long engagements over churn.** Many clients have been with Rakefire for 5-10+ years. We optimize for ongoing relationships where the client and the team get smarter together over time.
- **Pragmatic, boring tech.** Rails, Postgres, SQLite, Stimulus, Turbo. We rarely chase new frameworks unless they actually solve a problem. The tech is not the point — the working software is.
- **Senior team, small surface.** No layers of project managers between client and engineer. The person scoping the work is the same person writing the code.
- **Bootstrapped.** No outside investors. We pick clients and products that match our values, not what a board wants.

## Our SaaS products

### Causey — https://www.causey.app
Strategic planning software for nonprofits. Helps mission-driven organizations turn strategic plans into daily action with goal tracking, automated team check-ins, and board/funder reporting.

### Stoked — https://get.stokedhq.com
Peer-to-peer brand advocacy platform for DTC brands. Connects prospective buyers with real product owners through private SMS/email conversations.

Both are run by the Rakefire team. They share infrastructure, design language, and operating posture.
