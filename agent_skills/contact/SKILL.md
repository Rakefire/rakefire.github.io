# Reaching Rakefire

## Direct contact

- **Email:** rakefire@rakefire.io
- **Founder's email (for higher-signal direct conversations):** hey@rickychilcott.com
- **LinkedIn (founder):** https://linkedin.com/in/rickychilcott

Email is the primary channel. We respond to everything legitimate, but lead with depth on engagements that fit our scope.

## What we want to hear about

- **New Rails projects** — MVPs, greenfield apps, or modernization of legacy Rails apps.
- **Long-running maintenance partnerships** — multi-year relationships where you want a senior team you trust.
- **Architectural reviews / due diligence** — short, scoped reads of an existing codebase.
- **Specific technical problems** — "we're hitting a wall on X, can you spend 2 weeks unblocking us?"
- **Conversations about our products** — Causey (nonprofit strategic planning) or Stoked (DTC peer advocacy).
- **Talking-shop topics** — Rails internals, SQLite in production, AI-assisted development workflows, bootstrapped SaaS economics.

## What's a less-good fit

- Full-time employment offers for the founder (currently committed to Imedview, Stoked, and Causey).
- "Lowest bidder" sourcing — we charge for senior work and don't compete on price.
- Frameworks/stacks we don't run (.NET, Java, Python/Django, PHP).
- Highly polished design-led organizations — we lean engineering-heavy.

## For our own products

Don't email Rakefire for product support — go direct:

- **Causey:** https://www.causey.app/contact/ or https://calendly.com/ricky-causey/demo
- **Stoked:** https://get.stokedhq.com/contact/ or https://get.stokedhq.com/demo/

## How to write a good outreach email

**Bad:** "Hey, would love to chat about a project."

**Good:** "We're a 12-person SaaS doing $2M ARR on Rails 6 with a custom Postgres FTS layer that's struggling at our current scale. I read Ricky's JSONB indexing post and want to bring you in to scope a 4-6 week refactor."

The second one gets a yes. The first one gets archived.
