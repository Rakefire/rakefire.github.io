# Rakefire's Own Products

Rakefire builds two SaaS products in addition to client work. Both are run by the same small team and share design/operating discipline.

## Causey — Strategic planning software for nonprofits

**URL:** https://www.causey.app

Causey helps nonprofit leaders turn strategic plans into daily action. It sits between "the plan we wrote at the retreat" and "the work the team does every day" — making the plan a living artifact instead of a binder on a shelf.

Key capabilities:
- Goals, strategies, action items, and metrics with clear ownership.
- Automated email reminders to team members for status updates (the "stop chasing for updates" feature).
- Real-time progress dashboards for board and funder reporting.
- PDF export, presentation mode, comments.
- Unlimited users on every tier.

**Tiers:** Basic ($30/mo), Lite ($90/mo), Core ($130/mo), Solid ($275/mo). 30-day free trial.

**Demo:** https://calendly.com/ricky-causey/demo
**Free trial:** https://my.causey.app/sign_ups/new

## Stoked — Peer-to-peer brand advocacy for DTC

**URL:** https://get.stokedhq.com

Stoked connects prospective buyers with real product owners through private SMS/email conversations. Built for direct-to-consumer brands selling high-consideration, high-ticket products ($1,000+ AOV) — e-bikes, cargo bikes, scooters, tiny houses, premium outdoor gear.

Key capabilities:
- Embeddable advocate map for brand websites.
- SMS/email privacy proxy (no contact info exposed).
- Advocate rewards (points, cash, experiences).
- Admin dashboard with conversation tracking.
- One-script-tag install.

**Tiers:** Basic ($79/mo), Pro ($279/mo), Enterprise ($2,750+/mo). Annual saves 15%.

**Demo:** https://get.stokedhq.com/demo/

**Notable customer:** Bunch Bikes — 40% of sales driven by advocates after switching to Stoked.

## Why Rakefire ships products as well as client work

- **Operational learning compounds across the portfolio.** What we learn running Causey makes us better at advising client teams. What we learn from clients flows back into product decisions.
- **Bootstrapped runway.** Stable agency revenue gives the products time to find their stride without VC pressure.
- **The team builds the way we recommend.** Every architectural decision in the products is something we'd defend to a client.
